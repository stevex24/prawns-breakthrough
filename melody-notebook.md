# Checkmate Café — Melody Notebook

## Goal

Compose an original theme that listeners enjoy humming after hearing it.

The melody should become the musical identity of Checkmate Café,
not merely background music.

---

## Dramatic Structure

1. Welcome to the café
2. "I think I'll try the Prawn's Breakthrough."
3. Menu opens
4. Camera enters the menu
5. Chess animation
6. Return to the café

---

## Musical Character

- Original rather than imitative
- Warm
- Hum-able
- Light Italian flavor
- Cheerful without becoming comic parody
- Suitable for future Checkmate Café episodes

---

## Sources of Inspiration

- Gilda's restaurant
- Conversation with the Italian chess student
- Chess history
- Italian lyricism
- Renaissance atmosphere
